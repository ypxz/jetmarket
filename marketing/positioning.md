# Positioning — JetMarket vs the incumbents

> Basis: competitor table in `RESEARCH.md` §2 (pricing verified 2026-09-15).

## One-liner

**Charter, empty legs and aircraft for sale from vetted operators — request once,
get quotes, we take a fee only when it closes.**

## The market structure we're attacking

Private-aviation distribution splits into three incumbent classes, all of which
leave a hole:

| Class | Players | Model | The hole |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B broker/operator tools** | Avinode, CharterPad, Schedaero | Insider networks; fixed subscriptions (Avinode: $318/mo operator, $740–2,119/mo broker); gated by industry credentials | No public demand channel. Buyers can't see inventory; operators pay whether or not anything sells. |
| **Consumer charter apps** | XO (ex-JetSmarter), Lunajets | Buyer-side memberships/deposits ($250/mo + $50k deposit, or broker margin on every hour) | Monetize the buyer with walls; operators are invisible fulfillment supply behind the brand. |
| **Classifieds** | Trade-A-Plane, Controller, AvBuyer | Static paid ads ($50–300/mo/listing) | No RFQ, no quotes, no deal pipeline — a photo and a phone number. |

**The hole:** an *open, operator-first marketplace* — public storefronts for real
operator inventory, a structured RFQ→quote→deal pipeline, and pricing that scales
with results instead of punishing slow months.

## JetMarket position

- **Open, not gated.** Buyers browse, search, and send an RFQ free. Operators list
  free (3 listings) and see real demand before paying anything.
- **Operator storefronts, not a broker's black box.** Listings carry the operator's
  identity (with a visible verification badge), their photos, their price guidance.
  Contracts are between buyer and operator — we are the marketplace, not the broker.
- **Pay-on-results economics.** Pro is $199/mo — under the Avinode operator tier
  ($318/mo) — plus a success fee only when a deal closes: **3 % on charter,
  1.5 % on aircraft sales.** No deal, no fee.
- **High-ticket-native.** RFQ (route/dates/pax/budget) → fan-out to matching
  operators → comparable quotes → deal record → fee invoice. Built for $5k–$500k
  transactions, not a shopping cart.
- **Vertical by design.** The same engine re-skins for machinery and other
  high-ticket verticals — jets is proof one.

## Message house

**Roof:** *List your fleet where buyers actually look. Pay when it sells.*

- **Pillar 1 — Open demand:** buyers browse free; one RFQ reaches every operator
  whose fleet fits.
- **Pillar 2 — Aligned pricing:** $199/mo or free tier + 3 %/1.5 % success fee.
  A slow month costs nothing extra.
- **Pillar 3 — Trust without gatekeeping:** manual operator verification badge;
  marketplace-not-broker compliance; contracts between buyer and operator.

## Objection handling (operator sales)

- *"We already pay Avinode."* — Keep it for broker wholesale. JetMarket is the
  retail/public channel Avinode doesn't give you; it costs $0 to list.
- *"No escrow / instant booking?"* — Correct; we run RFQ→quotes→contract directly
  between buyer and operator. Stratajet proved fixed instant pricing is fragile;
  the industry still closes on quotes.
- *"Unverified listings everywhere?"* — Verification is a visible badge and an RFQ
  priority signal, not a paywall. Buyers see exactly who they're dealing with.

## Never claim

- Not a broker, not an operator, not an AOC holder. No Part-135/AOC claims except
  as operator-declared fields. No "vetted" claims for unverified operators.
